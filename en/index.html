03 / HOW I WORK

Step by step, with you.

You'll always know where your website stands, what's happening now and what comes next. No surprises.

  1. Meeting (30 min · free): You tell me about your business, your customers and what you need. By phone or video call, no strings attached.
  2. Proposal (Within a few days): I send you a fixed quote with what's included, the dates and the final price. We start with 50%.
  3. Design (1–4 weeks): I design your website with your brand and show you progress so you can see it take shape.
  4. Review (2 rounds of changes): We go through it together, calmly: copy, photos and details. I adjust whatever is needed.
  5. Delivery (Ready to use): I publish the website, show you how to manage it and you pay the remaining 50%. I'm still here if you need me.

07 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions.

How long does it take to get the website?

It depends on the package: Essential takes 1–2 weeks, Business about 3 and Booking about 4. I give you the exact dates in the proposal.

How do I pay?

Half when we start and the other half when the website is ready and you're happy with it. Prices are fixed: what the quote says is what you pay.

I don't have copy or photos. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I help you write the copy and tell you which photos are needed and how to take them with your phone. If you have none, we find quality photos that suit your business.

Can I make changes to the website later?

Yes. Every package includes rounds of changes before publishing. Afterwards, with maintenance (€29/month) you get up to 30 minutes of changes a month, or I quote them separately.

Will the website be mine?

Yes. The website, the copy and the photos are yours. If one day you want to move it elsewhere, I'll give you everything you need.

Do you only work in Tenerife?

I'm based in Adeje, in the south of Tenerife, but I work online with businesses all over Spain. Meetings are by phone or video call.

What is the booking system?

A calendar on your website where your customers choose a day and time themselves, at any hour. You get an email for every booking and manage everything from a private dashboard.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Of course. Book a call, we'll look at it together and I'll tell you what I'd change and how much it would cost, no strings attached.

Which languages do you speak?

I speak Spanish and English. The website is translated into several languages, but meetings are held in Spanish or English.
