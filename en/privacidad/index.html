01 / PRIVACY POLICY

Who is responsible

Mario Iglesias Martínez, based in Santa Cruz de Tenerife (Spain). Contact: hola@casaiglesias.es.

What data is collected and why

  • Booking a meeting: name, email, the type of meeting and, if you like, phone and topic. They are only used to organise the meeting, get in touch with you and send a reminder the day before. To prevent fake bookings, an anonymous fingerprint of your connection is also stored (not your IP address: it can't be worked out from the fingerprint).
  • Leaving a review: the name, business or sector and message you write and, if you like, your photo. They are published on this website after I check them, so don't include anything you don't want people to see. The photo is only uploaded if you tick the authorisation box, and you can ask me to remove it at any time.
  • Contact form and questionnaire: your name, email and whatever you write. It reaches me by email and I keep it only to reply to you and prepare your proposal.
  • Newsletter: your email, to send you one practical tip a month. Every newsletter includes a link to unsubscribe in one click.
  • CV builder: what you write is only stored in your own browser. It never reaches me or anyone else. The CV photo and logo are also stored only in your browser, already reduced in size.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box or sending your review. You can withdraw it whenever you want.

How long it is kept

Meeting data is kept for as long as needed to organise it and, at most, 12 months afterwards. Reviews stay published until you ask me to delete them.

Who else is involved

I don't sell or share your data. To make the website work I use these services, which only process the data on my behalf:

  • Supabase: database for bookings, messages and reviews, with servers in London (United Kingdom, a country with a level of protection recognised by the European Union).
  • Resend: sends the email notices (bookings, reminders, messages) and the newsletter (United States, with standard contractual clauses).
  • Behold and Instagram: if the home page shows my latest Instagram posts, the photos are loaded from their servers, which can see your IP address (United States).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website (United States).

The fonts, photos and other files are served from this website itself: while you browse, it doesn't connect to Google or other companies, except for the Instagram photos mentioned above.

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising cookies or third-party tools. It only stores in your browser what it needs to work: your CV draft (with your photo and logo, if you upload them), the questionnaire draft and whether you've already seen the intro animation.

To know how many people visit the website and how many come back, we keep our own statistics, which are not shared with anyone. We store a random number in your browser (it doesn't say who you are) and note the page viewed, the language of the website and of your browser, the website you came from (just its name), the type of device, the browser, the system, the screen size, the time zone and the approximate country. We don't store your IP; an encrypted fingerprint of the connection is used to stop abuse and is deleted after 30 days. Visits are deleted after 400 days. If your browser has «Do Not Track» turned on, your visit isn't counted; and if you clear this website's data in your browser, the number disappears.

Your rights

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, limit its use or object to it by writing to hola@casaiglesias.es. If you think I haven't handled it properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
